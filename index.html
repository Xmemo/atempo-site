Frequently Asked Questions

How does the 72-hour trial work? Does it auto-charge?

Never. Starting the voluntary 72-hour Pro trial requires no credit card and does not create a subscription. When the 72 hours end, you keep full offline access to Cyclic Sighing and Mindful Breathing without any automatic charge.

How do I restore my lifetime Pro purchase on a new iPhone or after reinstalling?

Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase, open atempo → Settings (or the Pro upgrade screen), and tap Restore Purchases.

Where is my practice history stored?

Your practice records, favorites, and preferences are stored locally on your device only. You can export a local JSON backup or restore one anytime in Settings → Local Data & Privacy.

Does atempo collect analytics or crash reports?

No. In atempo iOS v1, diagnostics and analytics are off by default with zero cloud user profiles, zero ads, and zero tracking.
