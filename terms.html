General wellness notice

atempo provides general wellness breathing and meditation exercises. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent disease. Stop a session if you feel pain, dizziness, breathlessness, or other discomfort, and seek qualified medical advice when appropriate.

Free access, 3-day trial, and lifetime purchase

atempo iOS v1 includes two permanently free practices (Cyclic Sighing and Mindful Breathing) that work offline without an account or payment.

atempo Pro unlocks the remaining four practices (Non-Sleep Deep Rest or NSDR, Resonance Breathing, Progressive Muscle Relaxation, and Loving-Kindness) through either the free $0 App Store 3-Day Trial (Non-Consumable, 72 hours, no auto-renewal) or a one-time US $9.99 (or local App Store equivalent) non-consumable lifetime purchase processed by Apple StoreKit. Purchases never auto-renew and can be restored anytime via Restore Purchases on the same Apple Account (expired 3-Day Trials do not re-grant trial access).

Users are responsible for using atempo in a safe setting. Do not use guided breathing while driving, operating machinery, in water, or in any situation requiring full attention.
