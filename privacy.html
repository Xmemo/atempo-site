atempo Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-17

atempo is a general wellness application for evidence-informed breathing and meditation practice. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Data stored on your device

atempo stores your language preference, practice history, favorites, and an internal random installation key locally on your device.

If you start a voluntary 72-hour Pro trial, the trial start and expiration timestamps are stored locally in Apple's iOS Keychain on your device so trial eligibility persists across reinstalls without creating an account.

An account is not required in atempo iOS v1. Cloud backup and account synchronization are not enabled. You can voluntarily export and restore a local JSON backup of your practice history at any time in Settings.

In-app purchases

If you choose to purchase atempo Pro (Lifetime), billing and purchase restoration are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit. atempo does not collect or store payment card details or Apple ID credentials.

Optional diagnostics and analytics

Anonymous analytics and crash diagnostics are off by default on fresh installations and are not exposed as an active tracking prompt or Settings toggle in the current iOS build. No analytics or crash reporting SDK initializes or transmits telemetry data unless explicitly enabled in a future opt-in release or diagnostic build.

If telemetry is ever enabled in a future opt-in configuration, atempo restricts events to bounded pseudonymous app-interaction events (such as practice selection, playback lifecycle, paywall views, or verified purchase/trial events), playback errors, crash logs, app version, build number, and a random installation identifier. Because a stable installation identifier links events from the same installation, it is pseudonymous rather than guaranteed unidentifiable.

This telemetry layer does not upload Coach answers, free-text notes, mood/journal entries, HRV or heart-rate values, health diagnoses, names, email addresses, contacts, microphone recordings, precise location, or advertising identifiers.

Offline audio playback

All guided and ambient audio tracks included in atempo iOS v1 are bundled locally on your device. Remote audio CDN downloads are disabled in this release, so sessions play offline without sending audio download requests.

Retention and deletion

Practice history is local in the current release and can be deleted in Settings.

Because the current release has no user account, cloud practice profile, or enabled telemetry, there is no remote profile or cloud practice data to delete.

Children and sensitive data

atempo is not designed to collect children's personal data. Do not submit health records or other sensitive personal information through support channels.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions can be sent to the developer support contact at gststhyzyus@gmail.com or via the support page at https://xmemo.github.io/atempo-site/ (also linked inside atempo and on its App Store product page). Material policy changes will be reflected by updating this document and its effective date.
